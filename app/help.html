Prefer to read it yourself instead? Here's a written guide.

The map

You'll see an interactive knowledge map with five levels — from broad domains (L1) down to individual concepts (L5). Drag and zoom the map, search for concepts in the top search box, and use the filters (e.g. grade band, subject) and layers on the left to make the map clearer.

Clicking a concept

Clicking a concept opens a side panel. Which buttons you see depends on its level:

  • L1 (domain): no buttons — pick a subtopic.
  • L2: "Learn this" shows a recommended learning order for its subtopics, with a "Sounds good" button that jumps to the first recommended one.
  • L3 and L4: "Set as goal" adds the topic to your goals. "Learn this" opens a structured learning path that walks step by step down to individual lessons.
  • L4 and L5: "Test me" starts a short 4-question diagnostic test.
  • L5 (individual concept): "Learn this" opens the lesson. The "I know this" toggle lets you mark that you already know it.

The lesson

Every lesson has four parts: explanation, example, practice, and meaning (why it matters). At any point you can say "too simple" or "too complex," ask for a different explanation, or ask questions directly inside the lesson via the ask bar.

Testing

The "Test me" button starts a 4-question adaptive diagnostic test that adjusts to your answers and gives you a read on how well you know the topic.

Goals and your Learner Passport

Goals, progress, achievements, points ("lumens"), and rank are all on your Learner Passport — open it from the menu (☰ icon, top right) under "Account." From there you can also link your account to a teacher or parent with an invite code so they can see your progress.

Notifications and settings

The same menu has Notifications (reminders and achievements) and Settings (font size, language, colour palette, focus timer, ambient sound).
